    Re-imagine a piece in another genre

    Pick a genre on the outer ring and choose how far to go with the inner ring or the Strength slider. Tap the centre to switch between the original and the styled version. Playback keeps its place when you switch.

    Models in the project

    • ACE-Step 1.5 Cover: a pre-trained diffusion transformer. Strength sets how much of the original structure it keeps.
    • LoRA adapters (M2): one small adapter per genre, trained on our classical and rock clips.
    • Mel-AE (M1): our autoencoder, with a shared encoder, one decoder per genre and a BigVGAN vocoder.
    • Style probe (M3): a genre classifier on CLAP / MERT embeddings that scores every output.

    About this demo

    Until our models produce outputs, the In the Hall of the Mountain King track (Grieg, 1875, public domain) is synthesised live in your browser to show how style switching works. It is not a model output. Mood currently shapes the tone in the browser; in the full system it becomes part of the prompt.
